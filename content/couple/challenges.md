# Couple challenges

- Send each other a photo of something blue within 10 minutes.
- Pay each other 3 genuine compliments today.
- No phones for the next 30 minutes of your next hangout.
- Each write down 5 things you're grateful for about the other.
- Learn one new fact about each other that you didn't know.
- Plan a tiny surprise for the other this week.
- Take a selfie in the silliest way you can and send it.
- Cook or order something the other loves, no questions asked.
- Send a voice note telling the story of your first meeting.
- Do a 10-minute walk or stretch together, even apart on a call.
