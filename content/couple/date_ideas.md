# Date ideas

- Cook a dish neither of you has ever made, with a video call if you're apart.
- Watch the same movie at the same time and text reactions.
- Build a shared playlist of 10 songs each, then rate each other's picks.
- Have a picnic — indoors counts.
- Take turns planning a 'surprise' mini date for the other.
- Go for a sunset walk and don't use your phones.
- Try a new café or dessert place together.
- Play board games and put a silly stake on the winner.
- Stargaze and make up constellations named after you two.
- Recreate your first date as closely as you can.
- Write each other letters to open in a year.
- Do a mini photo walk and pick each other's best shots.
- Learn five phrases of a new language together.
- Make a bucket list of 20 things for the next 5 years.
- Have a pyjama day with snacks and your favourite series.
