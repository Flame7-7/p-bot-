# Journal prompts

- Write about a moment with your partner that you never want to forget.
- What's something your partner did recently that made you feel loved?
- Describe a perfect ordinary day together.
- What do you hope the two of you are doing in five years?
- What's something you've learned from your partner?
- Write about a time you laughed together until it hurt.
- What are you most grateful for in this relationship?
- Write a note to your future selves.
